## What Changed
The New York State Department of Health has declared a new airborne infectious disease threat, triggering the activation of the HERO Act Airborne Infectious Disease Exposure Prevention Plans. This requirement is effective as of July 15, 2026, in accordance with the New York Health and Essential Rights Act (HERO Act), Labor Law Section 218-b.

## Who Is Affected
All private-sector employers in New York State, regardless of size, must comply with this requirement. This includes businesses with remote workers if the workers reside in New York.

## What Employers Should Do
Activate your HERO Act Airborne Infectious Disease Exposure Prevention Plan by July 15, 2026. Ensure all employees are informed of the plan's activation and trained on the specific measures outlined. Review and update the plan as necessary to address the current infectious disease threat.

## Key Deadlines
- **July 15, 2026**: Deadline to activate the HERO Act Airborne Infectious Disease Exposure Prevention Plan.

## Key Developments This Month

### California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) Amendments
The California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) has finalized amendments to the CPRA regulations, effective July 1, 2026. Key changes include expanded rights for employees regarding data access and correction. Employers with operations in California must update their privacy policies and employee notices to reflect these changes.

### New York Paid Family Leave Expansion
Effective July 1, 2026, New York's Paid Family Leave (PFL) program now includes coverage for siblings. Employers must adjust their leave policies to accommodate this expansion and ensure payroll systems are updated to reflect the new contribution rates.

## Compliance Deadlines

### EEO-1 Component 1 Report
The deadline for submitting the 2025 EEO-1 Component 1 data is July 31, 2026. Employers with 100 or more employees, or federal contractors with 50 or more employees, must file this report with the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC).

## Employer Action Items

1. **Update Privacy Policies**: For California employers, revise privacy policies and employee notices to comply with the CPRA amendments by July 1, 2026. Ensure that data access and correction procedures are clearly outlined.

2. **Adjust Leave Policies**: New York employers must update their family leave policies to include siblings as eligible family members under the PFL program by July 1, 2026. Communicate these changes to employees and update payroll systems accordingly.

3. **Submit EEO-1 Report**: Prepare and submit the EEO-1 Component 1 report by July 31, 2026. Ensure accurate data collection for the snapshot pay period.

## Looking Ahead

### IRS ACA Reporting Changes
The IRS has announced upcoming changes to the ACA reporting requirements for the 2026 tax year. Employers should anticipate updates to Forms 1094-C and 1095-C, with final guidance expected by October 2026. Prepare to adjust reporting processes in response to these changes.

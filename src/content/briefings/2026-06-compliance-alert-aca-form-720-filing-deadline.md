## What Changed

The deadline for filing IRS Form 720 to pay the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI) fee is June 30, 2026. This fee is mandated under the Affordable Care Act (ACA) and applies to certain health insurance policies and self-insured health plans.

## Who Is Affected

Employers sponsoring self-insured health plans, including Health Reimbursement Arrangements (HRAs), are required to pay the PCORI fee. This applies to employers of all sizes that provide these types of health plans.

## What Employers Should Do

Calculate the average number of lives covered under your self-insured health plan using one of the IRS-approved methods. Complete and file Form 720 with the IRS by June 30, 2026, and remit the appropriate PCORI fee payment.

## Key Deadlines

- **June 30, 2026**: Deadline to file Form 720 and pay the PCORI fee for plan years ending in 2025.

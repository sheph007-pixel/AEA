## Q: What are the ACA reporting requirements for the 2025 tax year?
Employers with 50 or more full-time employees must file IRS Forms 1094-C and 1095-C. The deadline for furnishing Form 1095-C to employees is January 31, 2026. For paper filing with the IRS, submit by February 28, 2026. If filing electronically, the deadline is March 31, 2026. Ensure your records are accurate and ready for these submissions.

## Q: How does the FMLA apply to remote workers?
Under 29 U.S.C. § 2611(2)(B)(ii), remote employees are covered by the FMLA if they report to a worksite with at least 50 employees within 75 miles. Review your remote workers' reporting locations to determine FMLA eligibility. Document any changes in reporting structures that might affect eligibility.

## Q: What is the deadline for submitting the EEO-1 Report?
The EEO-1 Report for 2025 must be submitted by July 31, 2026. Ensure you capture the snapshot pay period data between October 1 and December 31, 2025. Use this data to complete the Component 1 of the EEO-1 Report. Verify your employee counts and demographic information before submission.

## Q: Are there any changes to COBRA premium assistance in 2026?
As of July 2026, no new federal COBRA premium assistance extensions have been announced. Employers should continue to follow the standard COBRA requirements under 29 U.S.C. § 1161 et seq. for notifying eligible employees of their rights. Monitor any state-specific COBRA provisions that may apply to your business.

## Q: What are the current OSHA requirements for heat-related illnesses?
OSHA continues to enforce the General Duty Clause, 29 U.S.C. § 654, to protect workers from heat-related illnesses. Employers should implement heat illness prevention plans, including providing water, rest, and shade. Conduct training sessions for employees on recognizing and preventing heat stress, especially during summer months.

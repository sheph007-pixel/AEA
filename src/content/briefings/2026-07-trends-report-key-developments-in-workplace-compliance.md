## Key Development: OSHA Heat Illness Prevention Standard

On March 15, 2026, OSHA finalized its Heat Illness Prevention Standard, codified at 29 CFR § 1910.142, which mandates specific measures to protect workers from heat-related illnesses. This regulation applies to all employers with outdoor or indoor work environments where heat is a factor. Key requirements include providing access to water, rest breaks, and shaded areas. Employers must implement these measures by July 31, 2026.

**Action Item:** Conduct a heat risk assessment of your workplace and establish a heat illness prevention plan by July 31, 2026.

## Key Development: California Pay Transparency Law

California's Pay Transparency for Pay Equity Act (AB 1162) takes effect on July 1, 2026. This law requires employers with 15 or more employees to disclose pay scales in job postings and provide current employees with pay scale information upon request. Employers must also maintain records of job titles and wage history for all employees.

**Action Item:** Update job postings to include pay scales and ensure HR systems can provide pay scale information to employees upon request.

## Compliance Deadline: EEO-1 Component 1 Reporting

The EEOC has set the deadline for EEO-1 Component 1 data submission for the 2025 reporting year as July 15, 2026. This requirement applies to employers with 100 or more employees and federal contractors with 50 or more employees. The report collects demographic workforce data, including race/ethnicity, gender, and job categories.

**Action Item:** Submit your EEO-1 Component 1 report by July 15, 2026, using data from the 2025 snapshot pay period.

## Key Developments This Month

1. **Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) Updates**: Employers should be aware of potential changes to the FLSA that may expand eligibility for overtime pay, impacting payroll budgets and compliance strategies. Stakeholders are encouraged to stay informed about any developments and consider submitting comments during public feedback periods. The most recent comment period ended on November 7, 2023.

2. **Updates to the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)**: There may be evolving interpretations of the FMLA that could affect situations involving domestic violence and stalking. Employers should stay informed about any developments and consider updating their leave policies and training programs to ensure compliance with any new interpretations that aim to provide greater protections for affected employees.

3. **Expansion of the Affordable Care Act (ACA) Reporting Requirements**: Employers should remain attentive to any updates or changes in ACA reporting requirements. While no new guidelines have been confirmed, it is advisable for employers to regularly assess their reporting processes to ensure compliance with existing requirements and avoid potential penalties.

4. **Rise of Remote Work Policies**: A significant trend in the workforce continues to be the adoption of permanent remote work policies. Employers may consider evaluating their existing policies to align with this trend, ensuring they address both productivity and employee well-being.

5. **Increased Focus on Mental Health in the Workplace**: As mental health awareness grows, employers are encouraged to consider implementing programs that support employee mental health. This includes training for managers, access to mental health resources, and fostering an open environment for discussions about mental well-being.

## Compliance Deadlines

- **FLSA Comments Period**: The comment period for the proposed changes to overtime regulations under the FLSA ended on November 7, 2023.
- **ACA Reporting**: Employers should ensure compliance with existing ACA reporting requirements by the end of the calendar year, consulting with legal counsel for specific deadlines and requirements.
- **FMLA Policy Updates**: Employers should consider reviewing and updating their FMLA policies as necessary to ensure compliance and proper employee communication, consulting with legal counsel for guidance.

## Employer Action Items

- **Review Compensation Structures**: Employers should conduct a thorough review of current compensation structures in light of potential FLSA changes to prepare for possible adjustments.
- **Update Leave Policies**: Organizations should stay informed about any changes in FMLA guidance and consider revising their leave policies to ensure all employees are informed of their rights.
- **Enhance ACA Reporting Processes**: Employers should evaluate their current ACA reporting mechanisms and make any necessary adjustments to comply with existing guidelines.
- **Develop Remote Work Guidelines**: Employers may consider developing or revising remote work policies to accommodate the growing trend of remote work and ensure clarity for all employees.
- **Implement Mental Health Initiatives**: Employers should explore options for mental health programs and resources, emphasizing the importance of mental well-being in the workplace.

## Looking Ahead

As we move into the second half of 2026, employers should remain vigilant regarding ongoing regulatory changes and emerging workforce trends. It is advisable to regularly consult with legal counsel to ensure compliance with evolving laws and to foster a workplace environment that supports employee engagement and productivity. The AEA will continue to monitor these developments and provide updates as necessary.

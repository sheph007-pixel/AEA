## NLRB Updates Employee Handbook Guidelines

The National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) issued new guidance on May 20, 2026, clarifying the standards for evaluating employee handbook policies. This update affects employers nationwide and aims to ensure compliance with the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA), specifically focusing on policies that could be seen as restricting employees' rights under Section 7.

### Key Changes

The guidance outlines how the NLRB will assess whether handbook rules unlawfully interfere with employees' rights to engage in protected concerted activities. It emphasizes that policies should not be overly broad or ambiguous, as this could lead to interpretations that infringe on employees' rights to organize and discuss workplace conditions.

### Specific Areas of Concern

1. **Confidentiality Rules**: Employers must ensure that confidentiality policies do not prohibit employees from discussing wages, hours, or other terms and conditions of employment. The NLRB will scrutinize rules that broadly restrict such discussions.

2. **Civility Policies**: While maintaining workplace civility is important, policies should not be so broad as to restrict employees from expressing concerns about workplace issues. The guidance advises against using language that could be interpreted as prohibiting critical discussions about management or working conditions.

3. **Social Media Policies**: The NLRB highlights that social media rules must not infringe on employees' rights to engage in protected concerted activities. Policies should clearly define acceptable use without discouraging legitimate discussions about work-related matters.

### Employer Action Items

Employers should immediately review their employee handbooks to ensure compliance with the new NLRB standards. Key steps include:

- **Review and Revise Policies**: Audit current handbook policies, focusing on confidentiality, civility, and social media rules. Ensure that language is clear, specific, and not overly broad.

- **Consult Legal Counsel**: Engage with legal experts to interpret the NLRB guidance and apply it effectively to your specific workplace context.

- **Employee Training**: Educate HR teams and managers on the implications of the new guidance to prevent inadvertent violations of the NLRA.

### Implications for Employers

Failure to comply with the updated guidance could result in unfair labor practice charges. Employers should act promptly to align their policies with the NLRB's expectations, minimizing the risk of legal challenges and fostering a compliant workplace environment.

As the NLRB continues to refine its approach to employee handbook policies, staying informed and proactive in policy management is crucial for employers to maintain compliance and uphold employee rights.

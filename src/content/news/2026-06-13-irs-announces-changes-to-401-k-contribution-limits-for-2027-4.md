## IRS Increases 401(k) Contribution Limits for 2027

On June 12, 2026, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) announced an increase in the annual contribution limits for 401(k) plans, effective January 1, 2027. This change, outlined in IRS Notice 2026-45, raises the maximum elective deferral limit for employees from $22,500 to $23,500. The catch-up contribution limit for employees aged 50 and older will also increase from $7,500 to $8,000.

These adjustments reflect the IRS's response to inflationary pressures and are part of its annual review of contribution limits for retirement plans. Employers sponsoring 401(k) plans must update their payroll systems and communication materials to reflect these changes before the new limits take effect.

## Employer Action Items

Employers should begin by reviewing their current 401(k) plan documents to ensure compliance with the updated contribution limits. It is essential to communicate these changes to employees, highlighting the potential impact on their retirement savings strategy.

HR departments should update employee handbooks, benefits portals, and any other materials that reference 401(k) contribution limits. Additionally, payroll systems must be adjusted to accommodate the new limits, ensuring that employee contributions do not exceed the IRS thresholds.

## Impact on Retirement Planning

The increase in contribution limits offers employees an opportunity to enhance their retirement savings. Employers may consider hosting informational sessions or providing resources to help employees understand the benefits of maximizing their 401(k) contributions.

For employees nearing retirement, the higher catch-up contribution limit allows for additional savings during the final years of their careers. Employers should emphasize the importance of taking advantage of these increased limits to bolster retirement readiness.

## Compliance and Communication

Ensuring compliance with the new IRS limits is crucial to avoid potential penalties. Employers should coordinate with their plan administrators or financial advisors to verify that all plan documents and systems are updated accordingly.

Clear communication with employees is vital. Employers should provide detailed information about the new limits and encourage employees to review their contribution levels. This proactive approach can help employees make informed decisions about their retirement savings.

In summary, the IRS's increase in 401(k) contribution limits for 2027 presents an opportunity for both employers and employees to enhance retirement planning efforts. By taking the necessary steps to update systems and communicate changes, employers can support their workforce in maximizing retirement savings potential.

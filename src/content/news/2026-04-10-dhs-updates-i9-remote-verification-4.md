## The Rule

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has published a final rule making the alternative remote document inspection procedure for Form I-9 a permanent option for employers enrolled in E-Verify. The rule, effective May 1, 2026, codifies and updates the remote inspection process that was introduced as a temporary flexibility during the COVID-19 pandemic and subsequently extended through a series of interim rules.

Under the finalized procedure, qualifying employers may inspect I-9 identity and employment authorization documents remotely via live video interaction, rather than requiring in-person physical document inspection.

## Key Requirements

To use the remote inspection option, employers must meet the following conditions:

1. **E-Verify enrollment required**: Only employers who are enrolled in E-Verify and in good standing may use remote document inspection. Employers not enrolled in E-Verify must continue to conduct in-person inspection.

2. **Live video interaction**: Document inspection must occur during a live video call — static images, scanned copies, or pre-recorded video are not acceptable substitutes.

3. **Front and back copies**: Employers must obtain and retain clear copies of the front and back of all documents presented by the employee during the video inspection.

4. **E-Verify case submission**: Employers must create an E-Verify case for each employee whose documents are inspected remotely, within three business days of the employee's start date.

5. **Consistent application**: If an employer chooses to use remote inspection, it must apply the procedure consistently to all employees at a given location or within a defined workforce category. Selective use of remote inspection could raise discrimination concerns.

6. **Retention requirements**: Copies of remotely inspected documents must be retained alongside the Form I-9 for the required retention period.

## What This Means for Employers

The permanent remote inspection option is particularly significant for employers with distributed or remote workforces. Benefits include:

- **Simplified onboarding**: Remote employees can complete I-9 verification without traveling to a physical office or relying on authorized representatives
- **Reduced administrative burden**: Employers no longer need to coordinate in-person inspections for employees in different geographic locations
- **Consistency**: A single HR team can manage I-9 verification for the entire organization through a standardized remote process

## Action Items

- **Enroll in E-Verify** if you are not already enrolled and wish to take advantage of remote document inspection
- **Update your I-9 procedures** to incorporate the remote inspection option, including video technology requirements and document retention protocols
- **Train HR staff** on the specific requirements for remote inspection, including acceptable video formats and documentation standards
- **Review your consistency policies** to ensure remote inspection is offered uniformly and does not create the appearance of selective application based on protected characteristics
- **Update your I-9 forms**: DHS has released an updated Form I-9 that includes a checkbox for remote document inspection — ensure you are using the current version

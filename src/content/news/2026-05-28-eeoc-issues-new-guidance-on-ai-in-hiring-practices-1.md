## EEOC Guidance on AI Hiring Tools

The Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) issued new guidance on May 15, 2026, concerning the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in hiring practices. This guidance aims to ensure that employers using AI tools comply with existing anti-discrimination laws under Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.

AI-driven hiring tools are increasingly used by companies to streamline recruitment processes. However, these tools can inadvertently lead to discriminatory practices if not properly managed. The EEOC's guidance emphasizes that employers remain liable for any discriminatory outcomes, even if those outcomes are the result of AI algorithms.

## Key Points from the Guidance

The EEOC's guidance highlights several critical areas where employers must exercise caution:

1. **Bias in Algorithms**: Employers must ensure that AI tools do not disproportionately screen out candidates based on race, gender, or other protected characteristics. Regular audits of AI systems are recommended to identify and mitigate potential biases.

2. **Validation of Tools**: Employers should validate their AI tools to confirm they effectively measure job-related criteria. This involves demonstrating that the AI assessments are predictive of job performance and do not unfairly disadvantage any group.

3. **Transparency and Documentation**: Employers are advised to maintain detailed documentation of how AI tools are used in hiring decisions. This includes the criteria used by the AI and any adjustments made to reduce bias.

## Employer Action Items

Employers using AI in their hiring processes should take immediate steps to align with the EEOC's guidance:

- **Conduct Bias Audits**: Regularly audit AI systems to uncover and address biases. Use third-party experts if necessary to ensure objectivity.

- **Review Vendor Agreements**: If using third-party AI vendors, review contracts to include clauses that require compliance with anti-discrimination laws and provide access to audit results.

- **Train HR Teams**: Educate HR teams on the potential risks of AI in hiring and the importance of maintaining compliance with EEOC guidelines.

- **Update Policies**: Amend hiring policies to include procedures for the regular review and validation of AI tools.

The EEOC's guidance serves as a reminder that while AI can enhance efficiency, it must be used responsibly. Employers must balance technological advancements with their legal obligations to prevent discrimination in the workplace.

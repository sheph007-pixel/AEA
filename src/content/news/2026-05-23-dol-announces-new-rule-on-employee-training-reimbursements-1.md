## Mandatory Training Reimbursements Required by July 2026

The Department of Labor (DOL) has issued a new rule mandating that employers reimburse employees for expenses incurred during mandatory training sessions. This rule, published on May 15, 2026, under 29 C.F.R. § 785.27, will take effect on July 1, 2026. The regulation aims to ensure that employees are not financially burdened by the costs associated with mandatory training required by their employers.

## Who Is Affected?

This rule applies to all employers covered under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). It requires reimbursement for expenses such as travel, lodging, and materials when employees attend training sessions deemed mandatory by the employer. The rule does not cover optional training or professional development activities that are not directly required by the employer.

## Compliance Requirements

Employers must establish clear policies for reimbursing training-related expenses. This includes documenting what constitutes mandatory training and outlining the process for employees to submit expense claims. Failure to comply with these requirements may result in penalties under the FLSA.

### Action Items for Employers

1. **Review Training Programs**: Identify which training sessions are mandatory and communicate this clearly to employees.
   
2. **Update Reimbursement Policies**: Amend existing policies to include provisions for reimbursing travel, lodging, and material costs associated with mandatory training.

3. **Implement a Tracking System**: Establish a system to track training attendance and associated expenses to ensure accurate and timely reimbursements.

4. **Educate Employees**: Inform employees about the new reimbursement policy and how they can claim expenses.

## Potential Impacts

This rule is expected to increase operational costs for employers, particularly those with extensive training programs. However, it also aims to promote workforce development by removing financial barriers for employees. Employers should prepare for potential budget adjustments and consider the long-term benefits of a more skilled workforce.

## Conclusion

The DOL's new rule on mandatory training reimbursements underscores the importance of supporting employee development while ensuring compliance with federal regulations. Employers must act quickly to update their policies and processes before the July 1, 2026, deadline to avoid penalties and foster a compliant and supportive work environment.

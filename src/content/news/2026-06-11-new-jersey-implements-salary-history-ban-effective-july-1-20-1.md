## New Jersey's Salary History Ban

New Jersey's salary history ban takes effect on July 1, 2026, prohibiting employers from inquiring about a job applicant's salary history during the hiring process. This legislation, known as the New Jersey Salary History Ban Act (N.J. Stat. § 10:5-12.12), aims to promote pay equity by preventing the perpetuation of past salary discrimination.

Employers with operations in New Jersey must adjust their hiring practices to comply with the new law. Specifically, they are prohibited from asking about an applicant’s past compensation, including salary, wages, or benefits. The law also bars employers from requiring that an applicant’s salary history meet any minimum or maximum criteria.

## Implications for Employers

Employers should conduct a thorough review of their hiring policies and procedures to ensure compliance with the new legislation. This includes updating job application forms, interview scripts, and training hiring managers to avoid salary history inquiries. Employers should also ensure that any third-party recruiters or staffing agencies they work with are aware of and comply with the new requirements.

Violations of the New Jersey Salary History Ban Act can result in significant penalties. Employers may face fines of up to $10,000 for a first offense and up to $50,000 for subsequent offenses. Additionally, the New Jersey Division on Civil Rights may impose further sanctions for non-compliance.

## Recommended Actions

To align with the new law, employers should take the following steps:

1. **Audit Hiring Practices**: Review and revise all hiring documentation and procedures to remove any requests for salary history information.

2. **Train Staff**: Conduct training sessions for HR personnel and hiring managers to ensure understanding and compliance with the new law.

3. **Update Third-Party Agreements**: Communicate the new requirements to any external recruiters or staffing agencies and update contracts to reflect compliance obligations.

4. **Develop a Compliance Checklist**: Create a checklist for hiring processes to ensure no inadvertent violations occur during recruitment.

By preparing for these changes now, employers can avoid potential penalties and contribute to efforts toward pay equity. The New Jersey Salary History Ban Act represents a significant shift in hiring practices, emphasizing the importance of establishing equitable compensation structures based on the role and candidate qualifications rather than past earnings.

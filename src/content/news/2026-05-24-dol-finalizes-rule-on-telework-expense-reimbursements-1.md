## DOL Rule on Telework Expenses

The Department of Labor (DOL) finalized a rule on May 20, 2026, requiring employers to reimburse employees for necessary telework expenses. The rule, effective October 1, 2026, applies to all employers covered under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). This development mandates that businesses reimburse reasonable costs incurred by employees while working remotely, such as internet, phone, and office supplies.

## Key Details

Under the new rule, employers must cover costs directly related to telework that employees would not otherwise incur. These include internet service upgrades, additional phone lines, and office equipment necessary for job performance. The rule does not specify a reimbursement formula but requires that expenses be "reasonable and necessary" for the employee's duties.

The DOL's rule aligns with existing FLSA provisions, ensuring that employee reimbursements do not reduce earnings below the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour (29 U.S.C. § 206) or affect overtime calculations under 29 U.S.C. § 207.

## Employer Action Items

- **Audit Current Telework Policies:** Review and update telework agreements and policies to comply with the new reimbursement requirements. Ensure clarity on what constitutes reimbursable expenses.
  
- **Implement Reimbursement Procedures:** Establish a clear process for employees to submit expense claims. This should include guidelines on documentation and timelines for reimbursement.

- **Communicate Changes:** Inform employees about the new reimbursement policy and procedures. Provide training on how to submit expense claims effectively.

- **Budget Adjustments:** Anticipate potential increases in operational costs due to reimbursements and adjust budgets accordingly.

## Compliance and Risks

Failure to comply with the new rule can result in penalties under the FLSA, including back pay and damages. Employers should ensure that reimbursements are processed promptly to avoid disputes and potential legal challenges. Legal counsel may be advisable to navigate the intricacies of compliance and to address any specific concerns related to the rule.

This rule reflects the DOL's response to the growing prevalence of remote work and aims to standardize employer responsibilities regarding telework expenses. Employers must act swiftly to align their practices with the new requirements by the October deadline.

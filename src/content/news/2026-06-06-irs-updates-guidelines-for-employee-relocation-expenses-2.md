## IRS Clarifies Tax Treatment of Relocation Expenses

On May 30, 2026, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) issued updated guidelines on the tax treatment of employee relocation expenses. This clarification, detailed in IRS Notice 2026-32, affects how employers can reimburse employees for moving costs without triggering additional tax liabilities.

The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017 had suspended the exclusion for qualified moving expense reimbursements from 2018 through 2025. With this new guidance, the IRS provides clarity on how these reimbursements should be handled as the exclusion resumes in 2026.

## Key Changes and Employer Implications

The IRS guidance specifies that starting January 1, 2026, employers can again exclude qualified moving expenses from employees' taxable income, provided the expenses meet certain criteria. This includes costs for moving household goods and personal effects, as well as travel expenses related to the move, excluding meals.

Employers must ensure that relocation expense reimbursements align with IRS definitions of "qualified" expenses under 26 U.S.C. § 217. Non-qualified expenses, such as temporary housing or home-finding trips, remain taxable.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Review and Update Policies**: Employers should revise relocation reimbursement policies to reflect the reinstated tax exclusion. Ensure that reimbursement practices are compliant with IRS definitions of qualified expenses.

2. **Communicate with Employees**: Inform employees about the changes in tax treatment for relocation expenses. Transparency will help manage expectations and avoid confusion during the relocation process.

3. **Coordinate with Payroll Departments**: Ensure payroll systems are updated to correctly process relocation reimbursements as non-taxable income where applicable. This may involve software updates or manual adjustments to payroll processes.

4. **Consult with Tax Advisors**: Engage with tax professionals to ensure full compliance with the updated IRS guidelines and to optimize the tax benefits for both the company and its employees.

The updated IRS guidelines provide an opportunity for employers to offer more attractive relocation packages while maintaining compliance. By taking proactive steps, businesses can enhance their relocation policies and support talent mobility in a tax-efficient manner.

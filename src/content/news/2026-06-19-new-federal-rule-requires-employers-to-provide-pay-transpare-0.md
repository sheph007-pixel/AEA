## New Federal Rule on Pay Transparency

The U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) has finalized a rule requiring employers to include pay ranges in job postings, effective January 1, 2027. This rule applies to all employers with 15 or more employees and aims to promote pay equity and transparency in the hiring process.

The rule mandates that employers disclose the minimum and maximum salary or hourly wage for each position advertised. This requirement is part of the broader efforts under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) to address wage disparities and enhance transparency in compensation practices.

## Implications for Employers

Employers must now prepare to comply with this new requirement by auditing their current job postings and compensation structures. The rule affects all job advertisements, including those posted on online job boards, company websites, and recruitment platforms. Failure to comply could result in penalties and increased scrutiny from the DOL.

The rule also encourages employers to review their internal pay practices to ensure that the disclosed pay ranges accurately reflect the compensation offered to current employees in similar roles. This step is critical to avoid potential claims of wage discrimination or discrepancies that could arise from the newly mandated transparency.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Audit Job Postings**: Review all active and future job postings to ensure they include the required pay ranges. This includes positions advertised internally and externally.

2. **Update Compensation Policies**: Ensure that the disclosed pay ranges align with internal compensation policies and practices. Adjust any discrepancies to maintain consistency and fairness.

3. **Train HR and Recruitment Staff**: Provide training to HR and recruitment teams on the new requirements and the importance of maintaining accurate and transparent pay ranges in job postings.

4. **Consult Legal Counsel**: Engage with legal experts to understand the implications of the new rule and to ensure compliance with all aspects of the FLSA and other relevant regulations.

5. **Monitor for Updates**: Stay informed on any further guidance or updates from the DOL regarding the implementation of this rule.

By taking these steps, employers can ensure compliance with the new federal pay transparency rule and contribute to a more equitable workplace.

## FTC Enforces Stricter Rules on Subscription Services

On June 15, 2026, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) began enforcing new rules aimed at curbing deceptive practices in subscription services. These rules, part of the FTC's ongoing efforts to protect consumers, require businesses to provide clear and conspicuous disclosures about subscription terms and make it easier for consumers to cancel services.

The FTC's new regulations, under 16 C.F.R. Part 425, mandate that businesses must obtain express informed consent from consumers before charging them for subscription services. This includes clear communication of all material terms, such as billing frequency, renewal terms, and cancellation policies, before obtaining payment information.

## Impact on Employers

Businesses offering subscription-based products or services must immediately review their current practices to ensure compliance. Non-compliance could result in significant penalties, including fines and consumer redress. The FTC has made clear that enforcement will be rigorous, with particular focus on companies that fail to provide straightforward cancellation mechanisms.

Employers should train customer service teams to handle cancellation requests efficiently and ensure that any online cancellation processes are user-friendly and accessible. Additionally, marketing materials should be reviewed to confirm that all subscription offers are transparent and do not mislead consumers.

## Action Items for Compliance

1. **Review Subscription Agreements:** Audit all subscription agreements to ensure they meet the new FTC disclosure requirements. This includes clearly stating the cost, billing cycle, and cancellation process.

2. **Update Cancellation Processes:** Ensure that customers can easily cancel subscriptions through the same medium they used to subscribe. Online cancellations should be straightforward and not require unnecessary steps.

3. **Train Staff:** Conduct training sessions for customer service representatives to handle inquiries and cancellations in accordance with the new rules.

4. **Revise Marketing Materials:** Check all advertising and promotional materials for compliance with the FTC's transparency requirements to avoid misleading claims.

5. **Monitor Compliance:** Implement regular compliance checks and audits to ensure ongoing adherence to FTC regulations.

By taking these steps, businesses can avoid potential legal challenges and maintain consumer trust. As the FTC's enforcement actions begin, staying compliant will be crucial for businesses that rely on subscription models.

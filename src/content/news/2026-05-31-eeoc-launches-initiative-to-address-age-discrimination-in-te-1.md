## EEOC's New Focus on Age Discrimination

The Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) announced on May 30, 2026, a new initiative aimed at combating age discrimination in the technology sector. This move comes amid growing concerns about age bias in hiring practices within tech companies. The EEOC's initiative will involve increased scrutiny of hiring patterns, targeted investigations, and collaboration with industry leaders to promote age diversity.

### Background and Implications

Age discrimination complaints have been on the rise, particularly in industries like technology where younger workforces are often preferred. The EEOC's initiative is part of a broader strategy to enforce the Age Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967 (ADEA), which prohibits employment discrimination against individuals 40 years of age or older. The tech industry, known for its youthful culture, has faced criticism for practices that may inadvertently exclude older workers.

### What Employers Need to Know

The EEOC's focus will likely lead to more audits and investigations into tech companies' hiring practices. Employers should be prepared for potential inquiries and ensure that their recruitment processes comply with ADEA standards. This includes reviewing job advertisements, interview protocols, and selection criteria to eliminate age-related biases.

### Action Items for Employers

1. **Audit Hiring Practices**: Conduct a thorough review of recruitment and hiring processes. Ensure job descriptions and advertisements do not contain language that could be interpreted as age-biased.

2. **Training and Awareness**: Implement training programs for hiring managers and HR personnel to recognize and prevent age discrimination.

3. **Policy Updates**: Update company policies to explicitly prohibit age discrimination and outline procedures for handling complaints.

4. **Data Analysis**: Regularly analyze workforce demographics to identify potential age disparities and address them proactively.

By taking these steps, employers can mitigate the risk of age discrimination claims and foster an inclusive workplace that values diversity across all age groups.

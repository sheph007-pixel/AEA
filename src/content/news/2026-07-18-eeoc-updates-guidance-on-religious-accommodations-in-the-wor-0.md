## EEOC Issues New Guidance on Religious Accommodations

On July 15, 2026, the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) released updated guidance on religious accommodations in the workplace under Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964. This update provides clarity on employers' obligations to accommodate employees' religious practices unless doing so would cause undue hardship.

### Key Points of the Guidance

The new guidance emphasizes that employers must offer reasonable accommodations for employees' sincerely held religious beliefs, practices, or observances. It clarifies that "undue hardship" is defined as more than a de minimis cost or burden on the operation of the business. This standard was reaffirmed by the Supreme Court in the 2023 decision, Groff v. DeJoy.

The EEOC's guidance outlines examples of religious accommodations, such as flexible scheduling, voluntary shift substitutions, and modifications to workplace policies to allow religious attire or grooming. It also addresses common misconceptions, emphasizing that the accommodation process should be interactive and collaborative.

### Action Items for Employers

Employers should review their current policies and procedures regarding religious accommodations to ensure compliance with the updated EEOC guidance. Key actions include:

1. **Policy Review**: Ensure that your employee handbook clearly outlines procedures for requesting religious accommodations and the criteria for evaluating such requests.

2. **Training**: Conduct training sessions for HR personnel and managers to familiarize them with the new guidance and how to handle accommodation requests appropriately.

3. **Documentation**: Maintain thorough documentation of all accommodation requests and the interactive process undertaken to evaluate them, as this can be crucial in defending against potential claims.

4. **Interactive Process**: Engage in a meaningful dialogue with employees requesting accommodations to explore possible solutions that meet their needs without imposing undue hardship on the business.

### Implications for Employers

Failure to comply with the updated guidance could result in increased scrutiny from the EEOC and potential litigation. Employers should be proactive in addressing accommodation requests and documenting their efforts to comply with Title VII requirements.

By understanding and implementing these updates, employers can better navigate religious accommodation requests and foster an inclusive workplace environment. The EEOC's guidance serves as a reminder of the importance of respecting and accommodating diverse religious practices within the workforce.

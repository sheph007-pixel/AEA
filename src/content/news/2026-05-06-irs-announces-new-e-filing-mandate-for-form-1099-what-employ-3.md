## New IRS E-Filing Requirement

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) announced on April 30, 2026, that starting January 1, 2027, all employers filing 10 or more Form 1099s must submit them electronically. This change is detailed in the IRS's updated regulation under 26 U.S.C. § 6011(e), which aims to streamline the filing process and enhance data accuracy.

## Who Is Affected?

Businesses filing 10 or more information returns, including but not limited to Form 1099-MISC and Form 1099-NEC, will be required to e-file. This threshold is a significant reduction from the previous 250-form requirement, impacting small and medium-sized businesses that may not have previously needed to e-file.

## Compliance Steps for Employers

Employers must assess their current filing systems and prepare for the transition to electronic submissions. The IRS encourages utilizing the Filing Information Returns Electronically (FIRE) system, which facilitates the secure and efficient transmission of data.

### Action Items:

1. **Evaluate Current Filing Practices**: Determine if your business meets the new 10-form threshold.
   
2. **Upgrade Technology Systems**: Ensure your payroll or accounting software supports electronic filing. Consult with your software provider for necessary updates.

3. **Train Staff**: Educate your HR and payroll teams on the new e-filing process. Consider workshops or online training sessions to familiarize them with the FIRE system.

4. **Conduct a Trial Run**: Before the mandate takes effect, conduct a trial e-filing to identify and resolve potential issues.

## Potential Penalties

Failure to comply with the new e-filing requirement could result in penalties under 26 U.S.C. § 6721. Penalties start at $50 per return for late submissions, increasing to $280 per return if not corrected within 30 days. The IRS has indicated that penalties may be waived for the first year if good faith efforts to comply are demonstrated.

## Benefits of E-Filing

The IRS highlights several benefits of electronic filing, including faster processing times and reduced error rates. For employers, e-filing can lead to quicker refunds and less administrative burden in correcting errors.

## Conclusion

This e-filing mandate marks a significant shift in how small and medium-sized businesses handle tax reporting. By preparing now, employers can ensure compliance and take advantage of the efficiencies offered by electronic filing.

## IRS Increases 401(k) Contribution Limits for 2026

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) announced on May 10, 2026, an increase in the 401(k) contribution limits for the year 2026. The annual contribution limit for employees participating in 401(k) plans will rise to $23,000, up from the previous limit of $22,500. This change directly affects employers offering retirement savings plans and requires adjustments to payroll systems and employee communication strategies.

## Details of the Change

Under the Internal Revenue Code (IRC) § 402(g), the IRS adjusts the contribution limits annually based on cost-of-living increases. The new limit of $23,000 applies to traditional 401(k), 403(b), most 457 plans, and the federal government’s Thrift Savings Plan. Additionally, the catch-up contribution limit for employees aged 50 and over will increase from $7,500 to $7,750, allowing older workers to save more for retirement.

## Employer Action Items

Employers must update their payroll systems to accommodate the new limits effective January 1, 2026. This includes ensuring that contribution elections made by employees do not exceed the updated thresholds. HR departments should prepare to communicate these changes to employees, emphasizing the benefits of maximizing their retirement savings.

Employers should also review their plan documents and summary plan descriptions to ensure compliance with the new limits. This may involve consulting with plan administrators or legal counsel to verify that all documentation reflects the updated contribution rates.

## Communication Strategy

Effective communication is critical to ensure employees are informed about the changes and understand how they can benefit from increased contribution limits. Employers should consider hosting informational sessions or webinars to explain the benefits of maximizing contributions and the impact on long-term retirement savings.

Additionally, updating internal communication channels, such as newsletters or intranet postings, can help reinforce the message and encourage employees to review and adjust their contribution levels accordingly.

## Conclusion

The increase in 401(k) contribution limits for 2026 offers employees an opportunity to enhance their retirement savings. Employers must take proactive steps to update systems, communicate changes, and ensure compliance with IRS regulations. By doing so, they not only fulfill their legal responsibilities but also support their workforce in achieving better financial security in retirement.

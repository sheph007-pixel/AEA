## FTC's New Guidelines on Deceptive Pricing

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) released new guidelines on May 25, 2026, aimed at curbing deceptive pricing practices in retail and e-commerce sectors. These guidelines, part of the FTC's ongoing efforts under Section 5 of the FTC Act (15 U.S.C. § 45), emphasize transparency in advertised prices and discount claims.

According to the guidelines, businesses must ensure that any advertised discounts are genuine and reflect a true reduction from the regular price. The FTC highlighted that inflated original prices or misleading discount claims could lead to enforcement actions. This move comes as part of a broader initiative to protect consumers from misleading sales tactics and enhance fair competition.

## Key Implications for Employers

Retailers and e-commerce companies need to review their pricing strategies and marketing communications to comply with these guidelines. The FTC's focus includes scrutinizing "was" and "now" price comparisons and ensuring that any reference to a "regular" price is accurate and was offered for a reasonable period.

The guidelines also address the use of "limited-time offers" and "flash sales," which must be substantiated with clear evidence of the offer's temporary nature. Businesses found in violation could face significant penalties, including fines and mandatory corrective advertising.

## Action Items for Compliance

Employers in the affected sectors should immediately audit their current pricing and promotional strategies. Key steps include:

1. **Review Historical Pricing Data:** Ensure that advertised discounts are based on actual prices previously offered for a substantial period.
   
2. **Update Marketing Materials:** Verify that all promotional content accurately reflects pricing claims and is free from misleading language.
   
3. **Train Marketing Teams:** Conduct training sessions for marketing and sales teams to understand the new guidelines and the importance of compliance.
   
4. **Implement Monitoring Systems:** Establish internal controls to regularly review pricing practices and promotional materials for adherence to FTC standards.

By taking these steps, businesses can mitigate the risk of FTC scrutiny and potential penalties. As the FTC increases its oversight in the retail and e-commerce sectors, maintaining transparent and honest pricing practices is crucial for sustaining consumer trust and avoiding legal complications.

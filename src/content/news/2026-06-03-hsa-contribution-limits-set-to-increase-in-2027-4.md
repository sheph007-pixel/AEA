## IRS Announces Increased HSA Contribution Limits for 2027

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) announced on May 25, 2026, that Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution limits will increase for the 2027 tax year. This adjustment, outlined in IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24, affects both individuals and families participating in high-deductible health plans (HDHPs).

For 2027, individuals with self-only coverage under an HDHP will be able to contribute up to $4,150 to their HSA, up from the current limit of $4,050. The contribution limit for those with family coverage will rise to $8,300, an increase from $8,100. These changes reflect the IRS's annual adjustment based on inflation.

## Impact on Employers and Employees

Employers offering HSAs as part of their benefits packages should prepare to update their systems to accommodate these new limits. This adjustment provides an opportunity to enhance employee engagement by communicating the benefits of maximizing HSA contributions, which include tax-free savings for medical expenses and potential retirement savings growth.

Employees, meanwhile, can benefit from reassessing their contribution strategies to take full advantage of the tax benefits associated with HSAs. Employers should consider holding informational sessions or providing resources to help employees understand how these accounts can be used effectively.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Update Payroll Systems**: Ensure your payroll and benefits systems reflect the new contribution limits for the 2027 tax year. This will prevent any discrepancies in employee contributions and ensure compliance with IRS regulations.

2. **Communicate Changes**: Inform employees of the new limits and the potential benefits of increased contributions. Consider distributing a memo or hosting a webinar to explain how HSAs can be used to manage healthcare costs and save for the future.

3. **Review Plan Design**: Evaluate your current HSA offerings and consider whether adjustments to employer contributions or other plan features could enhance your benefits package. Aligning benefits with employee needs can improve satisfaction and retention.

4. **Provide Education**: Offer educational resources or workshops on the advantages of HSAs, including tax savings and long-term financial planning. Employees who understand these benefits are more likely to participate and contribute at higher levels.

By proactively addressing these changes, employers can ensure compliance and enhance the value of their benefits offerings. The increased HSA contribution limits present an opportunity to support employees in managing healthcare expenses and planning for their financial futures.

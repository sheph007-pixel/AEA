## FTC Issues New Guidelines on Subscription Practices

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) released new guidelines on July 10, 2026, targeting deceptive subscription practices. These guidelines, known as the "Click to Cancel" rule, are aimed at businesses that offer subscription services to ensure transparency and fairness in consumer transactions. The rule is part of the FTC's ongoing effort to combat "dark patterns" that can mislead consumers into unwanted subscriptions or make cancellation difficult.

## Key Provisions of the "Click to Cancel" Rule

The "Click to Cancel" rule mandates that businesses must provide a simple, straightforward method for consumers to cancel subscriptions. Specifically, the cancellation process must be as easy as the process to sign up. This includes offering an online cancellation option if the subscription was initiated online, per FTC's guidelines under 16 C.F.R. Part 425.

Additionally, businesses must obtain express consent from consumers before charging them for a subscription and must send a clear notification before any automatic renewal. These steps are intended to prevent consumers from being charged for services they do not wish to continue.

## Implications for Employers

Employers in sectors offering subscription-based services, such as software, media, and e-commerce, need to immediately review their subscription processes to ensure compliance with the new rule. Non-compliance can result in significant penalties, including fines and legal action by the FTC.

### Action Items for Employers

1. **Audit Subscription Processes**: Conduct a thorough review of the current subscription sign-up and cancellation procedures to ensure they meet the new FTC requirements.
   
2. **Update Online Platforms**: Ensure that online platforms provide an easy-to-find and straightforward cancellation option, mirroring the simplicity of the sign-up process.

3. **Revise Consumer Communications**: Update consumer communication templates to include clear information about subscription terms, renewal notices, and cancellation procedures.

4. **Training for Customer Service Teams**: Train customer service representatives to handle cancellation requests efficiently and in accordance with the new guidelines.

5. **Monitor Compliance**: Establish a monitoring system to regularly check compliance with the FTC's guidelines and adjust practices as necessary.

## Looking Ahead

The FTC's increased scrutiny on subscription practices signals a broader regulatory trend towards consumer protection. Businesses should anticipate further regulatory developments in this area and be prepared to adapt quickly to new requirements. The "Click to Cancel" rule is a crucial step in ensuring consumer rights are upheld, and compliance will not only prevent legal repercussions but also enhance consumer trust and satisfaction.

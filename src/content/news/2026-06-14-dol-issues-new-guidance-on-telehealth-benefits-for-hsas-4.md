## DOL Clarifies Telehealth and HSA Eligibility

On June 1, 2026, the Department of Labor (DOL) issued new guidance clarifying the eligibility of telehealth services for Health Savings Account (HSA)-qualified high-deductible health plans (HDHPs). This guidance, outlined in DOL Bulletin 2026-05, addresses how telehealth services can be integrated into HDHPs without disqualifying the plan from HSA eligibility.

Under the Internal Revenue Code Section 223(c)(2), HDHPs must meet specific criteria, including minimum deductible requirements, to qualify for HSA participation. The DOL's recent clarification allows telehealth services to be covered before the deductible is met, without affecting the plan's status as an HSA-qualified HDHP. This change is effective immediately and applies to plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2027.

## Implications for Employers

Employers offering HDHPs with HSAs should review their plan documents and telehealth service offerings to ensure compliance with the new DOL guidance. This development provides an opportunity to enhance employee benefits by expanding access to telehealth services without jeopardizing HSA eligibility.

Action items for employers include:

- **Review Plan Documents**: Ensure that HDHPs are updated to reflect the inclusion of telehealth services before the deductible is met.
- **Communicate Changes**: Inform employees about the expanded telehealth benefits and how they interact with HSA contributions.
- **Coordinate with Insurers**: Work with insurance providers to integrate telehealth services into existing HDHPs in compliance with the new guidance.

## Enhancing Employee Benefits

The inclusion of telehealth services in HDHPs offers significant advantages for both employers and employees. Employers can provide a more attractive benefits package, which can aid in recruitment and retention efforts. Employees benefit from increased access to healthcare services, especially in remote or underserved areas, without the burden of meeting high deductibles first.

By aligning telehealth services with HDHPs, employers can support employee wellness while maintaining the tax-advantaged status of HSAs. This move reflects a broader trend towards integrating digital health solutions into employee benefits, offering flexibility and accessibility in healthcare options.

For further details, employers can access the full text of DOL Bulletin 2026-05 on the Department of Labor's website.

## FTC Updates Influencer Marketing Guidelines

On June 15, 2026, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) released new guidelines regarding influencer marketing disclosures. These guidelines, outlined in the updated "Endorsement Guides," aim to ensure transparency and honesty in advertising. Businesses utilizing influencers for marketing must comply with these guidelines to avoid potential penalties.

The new guidelines emphasize the importance of clear and conspicuous disclosures when influencers promote products or services. The FTC mandates that any material connection between an influencer and a brand must be disclosed in a manner that is easily noticeable and understandable by the average consumer. This includes financial compensation, free products, or any other incentives provided to influencers.

## Key Changes and Requirements

The updated guidelines specify that disclosures should not be buried in hashtags or hidden in lengthy captions. Instead, they must be placed in a location where they are immediately visible, such as at the beginning of a post or within the first few seconds of a video. The FTC also advises against ambiguous language, recommending straightforward terms like "ad" or "sponsored" to convey the relationship between the influencer and the brand.

Additionally, the guidelines address the use of platforms with character limits, such as Twitter. In these cases, the FTC requires that disclosures are still made clearly, even if it means using abbreviated terms that are widely understood.

## Implications for Businesses

Businesses that rely on influencer marketing should review their current contracts and agreements with influencers to ensure compliance with the new guidelines. Non-compliance could lead to enforcement actions by the FTC, including fines and reputational damage.

Employers should take the following actions:
1. **Audit Current Influencer Agreements**: Review all existing influencer partnerships to ensure that disclosure practices align with the updated FTC guidelines.
2. **Update Contracts**: Amend contracts to include specific language about disclosure requirements and the consequences of non-compliance.
3. **Training and Education**: Provide training for marketing teams and influencers on the importance of proper disclosures and the new requirements.
4. **Monitor Compliance**: Establish a system to regularly monitor influencer content for compliance with FTC guidelines.

By taking these steps, businesses can protect themselves from potential legal issues and maintain consumer trust through transparent marketing practices.

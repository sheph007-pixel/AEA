## DOL Clarifies Telehealth Certification for FMLA

The Department of Labor (DOL) has issued new guidance clarifying the use of telehealth services for certifying Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) leave, effective July 1, 2026. This update allows healthcare providers to certify FMLA leave through telehealth consultations, expanding the options for employees to obtain necessary documentation.

Under the revised regulations, telehealth visits must meet specific criteria to qualify for FMLA certification. The telehealth consultation must be conducted by a healthcare provider who is authorized to practice in the state and must involve a real-time, interactive audio and video connection. Simple phone calls or text messages do not suffice. This change is part of the ongoing adaptation to the increased use of telehealth services in the healthcare industry.

## Implications for Employers

Employers must update their FMLA policies to reflect this change. It is crucial to ensure that HR teams and managers are aware of the criteria for telehealth certifications and adjust their processes accordingly. Training sessions or informational updates may be necessary to communicate these changes effectively.

Additionally, employers should review their current FMLA forms and documentation procedures to accommodate telehealth certifications. This includes updating any internal systems or software that track FMLA leave to ensure compliance with the new regulations.

## Action Items

1. **Update FMLA Policies**: Revise your company’s FMLA policies to include telehealth certification as a valid option for employees seeking leave.
   
2. **Train HR Teams**: Conduct training sessions for HR personnel and managers to familiarize them with the new telehealth certification requirements.

3. **Review Documentation Procedures**: Ensure that your FMLA forms and tracking systems are updated to accept telehealth certifications.

4. **Communicate Changes**: Inform employees about the new option for telehealth certification to ensure they understand how to obtain FMLA leave under the updated rules.

By taking these steps, employers can ensure they remain compliant with the updated FMLA regulations and continue to support their employees effectively.

## IRS Proposes Clarification on Remote Work Equipment Deductions

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has proposed a new rule to clarify the tax treatment of remote work equipment expenses. Announced on May 27, 2026, this proposal aims to provide clearer guidelines on what remote work-related costs can be deducted by employers and employees alike. This development comes as remote work remains prevalent across various industries.

### Key Details of the Proposal

The proposed rule seeks to amend existing regulations under the Internal Revenue Code, specifically focusing on Section 162, which governs the deduction of business expenses. The IRS intends to outline which equipment expenses qualify for deductions, such as computers, office furniture, and internet services, when used primarily for business purposes.

Under the proposed rule, employers who provide equipment to employees for remote work would be able to deduct the full cost of these items as business expenses. Employees, however, would need to demonstrate that the expenses are necessary and directly related to their work duties to qualify for deductions on their personal tax returns.

### Implications for Employers

Employers should review their current policies on remote work equipment to ensure compliance with the proposed rule. Businesses may need to consider formalizing reimbursement policies for remote work equipment to facilitate proper deductions. Additionally, maintaining clear records of equipment purchases and allocations will be essential for substantiating deductions.

### Next Steps

The IRS is accepting public comments on the proposed rule until July 31, 2026. Employers and HR professionals are encouraged to review the proposal and provide feedback. The final rule is expected to be issued later this year, potentially impacting tax filings for the 2026 tax year.

In preparation, businesses should evaluate their current remote work setups and consider adjustments to align with the anticipated regulatory changes. This proactive approach will help ensure that companies can maximize their tax benefits while remaining compliant with IRS regulations.

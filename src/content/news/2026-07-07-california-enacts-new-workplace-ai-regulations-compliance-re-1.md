## New AI Regulations in California

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed the California AI Accountability Act into law on June 30, 2026. This new legislation, effective January 1, 2026, regulates the use of artificial intelligence in hiring and other HR processes. Companies operating in California must comply with specific transparency and fairness requirements when utilizing AI tools in employment decisions.

## Key Provisions

The Act mandates that employers disclose the use of AI in hiring processes to applicants. Employers must also provide an explanation of how AI tools influence employment decisions. This includes detailing the data inputs and algorithms used. Additionally, companies must conduct regular audits to ensure AI systems do not produce discriminatory outcomes based on race, gender, age, or other protected characteristics.

## Compliance Requirements

Employers with operations in California need to prepare for these changes by:

1. **Reviewing AI Systems**: Conduct an audit of current AI tools used in hiring and HR processes to assess compliance with the new law. Ensure that these systems are free from biases that could lead to discrimination.

2. **Updating Applicant Communications**: Amend job postings and application processes to include disclosures about AI usage. Clearly explain how AI will be used in evaluating candidates.

3. **Training HR Staff**: Educate HR teams on the new requirements and ensure they are equipped to manage AI tools in accordance with the law. This includes understanding how to interpret AI outputs and making informed decisions.

4. **Documenting Compliance Efforts**: Maintain records of AI audits and any corrective actions taken to address potential biases. This documentation will be crucial in demonstrating compliance during any regulatory reviews.

## Implications for Employers

Failure to comply with the California AI Accountability Act can result in penalties and legal challenges. Employers should act swiftly to align their AI practices with the new law. Beyond compliance, these efforts can enhance the fairness and transparency of hiring processes, potentially improving candidate trust and satisfaction.

The California AI Accountability Act reflects a growing trend toward regulating AI in the workplace. Employers across the U.S. should anticipate similar regulations emerging in other states, emphasizing the importance of proactive compliance strategies.

## EEOC Issues Guidance on AI in Performance Reviews

On May 12, 2026, the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) released new guidance addressing the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in employee performance reviews. This guidance is crucial for employers using AI tools to evaluate employee performance, as it clarifies compliance with Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, which prohibits employment discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.

The EEOC's guidance emphasizes that employers must ensure AI systems do not result in disparate impact discrimination. Disparate impact occurs when a facially neutral employment practice disproportionately affects a protected group. Employers using AI in performance reviews must validate that their systems are free from bias and do not inadvertently disadvantage any group.

## Key Compliance Requirements

Employers are required to conduct regular audits of their AI systems to ensure compliance with Title VII. These audits should assess whether the AI tools are consistently and fairly evaluating all employees, regardless of their protected characteristics. The EEOC suggests that employers engage external experts to review AI algorithms and their impact on different demographic groups.

Additionally, the guidance advises employers to provide transparency to employees regarding how AI is used in performance evaluations. Employees should be informed about the data being collected, how it is used, and the criteria for evaluation. This transparency can help mitigate concerns and foster trust in the use of AI technologies.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Conduct AI Audits**: Employers should immediately begin auditing their AI-based performance review systems. This includes evaluating the algorithms for potential bias and ensuring they comply with Title VII requirements.

2. **Engage Experts**: Consider hiring external experts to validate AI systems. These experts can provide an unbiased assessment of whether the AI tools are fair and compliant with anti-discrimination laws.

3. **Enhance Transparency**: Communicate with employees about the use of AI in performance reviews. Explain the purpose, data collection methods, and evaluation criteria to ensure employees understand and trust the process.

4. **Training and Development**: Train HR and management staff on the implications of the EEOC guidance. Ensure they understand how to implement and monitor AI systems effectively and in compliance with legal standards.

## Implications for Employers

The EEOC's new guidance underscores the importance of using AI responsibly in the workplace. As AI becomes increasingly integrated into HR practices, employers must be diligent in ensuring their systems do not inadvertently violate anti-discrimination laws. Failure to comply with these guidelines could result in legal challenges and damage to company reputation.

Employers should view this guidance as an opportunity to enhance their AI systems, ensuring they are both effective and equitable. By taking proactive steps now, businesses can avoid potential pitfalls and leverage AI to improve performance evaluations in a fair and compliant manner.

## New Rule Enforces Swift Action on Data Breaches

A new federal rule requiring employers to notify employees of data breaches affecting their personal information took effect on June 1, 2026. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) issued the rule under the Data Breach Notification Act (15 U.S.C. § 6801 et seq.), mandating that affected employees be informed within 30 days of a breach's discovery.

## Scope and Requirements

This regulation applies to all employers who handle employee personal data, including social security numbers, financial account details, and health information. Employers must provide a clear and conspicuous notice to each affected individual, detailing the nature of the breach, the types of information compromised, and steps the company is taking to address the incident.

The rule also requires that employers inform the FTC if a breach affects more than 500 individuals. Failure to comply can result in significant penalties, including fines up to $50,000 per violation.

## Action Items for Employers

Employers should immediately review and update their data security policies and incident response plans. Key steps include:

- **Audit Data Security Measures:** Ensure robust encryption and access controls are in place to protect employee data.
- **Develop a Breach Response Plan:** Outline procedures for identifying, reporting, and mitigating data breaches.
- **Train Employees:** Conduct mandatory training sessions on data protection and breach response protocols.
- **Partner with IT Security Experts:** Engage cybersecurity professionals to evaluate and enhance your data protection strategies.

## Legal and HR Implications

HR departments must coordinate closely with IT and legal teams to ensure compliance with the new rule. This includes maintaining accurate records of data breaches and notifications, which could be subject to audit by the FTC. Employers should also consider offering credit monitoring services to affected employees as a goodwill gesture to mitigate potential fallout.

## Conclusion

The new data breach notification rule underscores the importance of safeguarding employee information. Employers must act swiftly to align their practices with federal requirements, minimizing risks and demonstrating a commitment to data security.

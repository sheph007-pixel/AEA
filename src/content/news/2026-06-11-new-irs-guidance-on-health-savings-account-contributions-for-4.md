## IRS Raises HSA Contribution Limits

The IRS announced on June 1, 2026, an increase in Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution limits for 2027. This adjustment, outlined in IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24, affects both individual and family coverage under high-deductible health plans (HDHPs). The new limits allow individuals to contribute up to $4,150, while those with family coverage can contribute up to $8,300. This represents an increase from the 2026 limits of $4,050 for individuals and $8,100 for families.

## Impact on Employers

Employers offering HDHPs with HSAs should update their payroll systems to reflect the new contribution limits. This change will require adjustments to employee benefit communications and potentially to automatic payroll deductions for HSA contributions. Employers should ensure that their benefits administration systems are configured to accommodate these higher limits by January 1, 2027.

## Employee Communication Essential

HR professionals must communicate these changes clearly to employees, emphasizing the potential tax advantages and savings opportunities associated with maximizing HSA contributions. Given the tax-free growth and tax-free withdrawals for qualified medical expenses, HSAs remain a valuable tool for employees to manage healthcare costs.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Update Payroll Systems**: Ensure that payroll and benefits systems are updated to allow for increased HSA contributions starting January 1, 2027.
   
2. **Review Benefits Communication**: Revise employee communications to highlight the new contribution limits and the benefits of HSAs.
   
3. **Coordinate with Benefits Providers**: Confirm that third-party benefits administrators are aware of the new limits and are prepared to implement the changes.

4. **Consider Employer Contributions**: Evaluate whether to adjust employer contributions to HSAs in light of the increased limits, potentially enhancing the attractiveness of your benefits package.

Employers should act promptly to implement these changes, ensuring compliance and maximizing the value of HSAs for their workforce.

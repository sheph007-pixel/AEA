## New IRS Mileage Rate for 2026

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has announced the standard mileage rate for business travel in 2026, set at 66 cents per mile. This rate, effective January 1, 2026, reflects an increase from the 2025 rate of 65.5 cents per mile. The adjustment accounts for fluctuations in fuel prices and vehicle maintenance costs, impacting reimbursement calculations for employee travel expenses.

## Implications for Employers

Employers utilizing the IRS standard mileage rate for reimbursing employees should update their expense policies to align with the new rate. This change affects all businesses that reimburse employees for business-related driving using personal vehicles. The updated rate helps ensure employees are fairly compensated for their travel costs, maintaining compliance with IRS guidelines.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Update Reimbursement Policies:** Employers should revise their mileage reimbursement policies to reflect the new 66 cents per mile rate starting January 1, 2026. This ensures consistency in expense reporting and compliance with IRS standards.

2. **Communicate Changes:** Inform employees about the updated mileage rate and how it affects their travel reimbursements. Clear communication helps avoid confusion and ensures employees submit accurate expense claims.

3. **Adjust Budget Projections:** Review and adjust budget forecasts to accommodate the increased reimbursement rate. This will help manage travel expenses effectively throughout the fiscal year.

4. **Review Mileage Logs:** Encourage employees to maintain detailed and accurate mileage logs. Proper documentation supports reimbursement claims and ensures compliance with IRS record-keeping requirements.

By taking these steps, employers can smoothly transition to the new mileage rate and maintain compliance with IRS regulations.

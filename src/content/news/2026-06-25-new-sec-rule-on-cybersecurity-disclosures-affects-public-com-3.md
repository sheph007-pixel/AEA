## SEC Mandates Cybersecurity Risk Disclosures

The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) finalized a new rule on June 15, 2026, requiring public companies to disclose detailed information about their cybersecurity risk management, strategy, and governance. This rule, effective December 1, 2026, aims to enhance transparency and protect investors by ensuring they have access to critical information regarding a company's cybersecurity posture.

## Key Requirements

Under the new rule, companies must include cybersecurity disclosures in their annual reports (Form 10-K) and certain registration statements. Specifically, the rule mandates that companies disclose:

1. **Cybersecurity Risk Management**: A description of the company's policies and procedures for identifying and managing cybersecurity risks, including any board-level oversight.

2. **Governance**: Information about the board of directors' oversight of cybersecurity risk, including the board's expertise in cybersecurity matters.

3. **Cyber Incidents**: Disclosure of material cybersecurity incidents, including the nature, scope, and impact of such incidents on the company's operations and financial condition.

## Impact on Employers

While the rule primarily targets public companies, private companies should also take note, as cybersecurity diligence is becoming a standard expectation among investors and business partners. Companies should evaluate their current cybersecurity frameworks and ensure they align with the new disclosure requirements.

## Action Items for Employers

- **Review and Update Cybersecurity Policies**: Public companies must review their existing cybersecurity policies and procedures to ensure compliance with the new SEC requirements. This includes documenting risk management strategies and board oversight mechanisms.

- **Enhance Board Expertise**: Consider adding board members with cybersecurity expertise or providing existing board members with training to enhance their understanding of cybersecurity risks and governance.

- **Prepare Disclosure Reports**: Start compiling necessary information for the upcoming disclosures, focusing on past cybersecurity incidents and the company's response effectiveness.

- **Engage with Legal and Compliance Teams**: Work closely with legal and compliance teams to ensure all disclosures meet SEC requirements and are accurately reflected in official filings.

## Looking Ahead

The SEC's new rule reflects an increasing regulatory focus on cybersecurity, emphasizing its importance in business operations and investor protection. Companies should act promptly to align with these requirements, as failure to comply could result in regulatory scrutiny and potential penalties. This development underscores the critical role of cybersecurity in corporate governance and the need for robust risk management strategies.

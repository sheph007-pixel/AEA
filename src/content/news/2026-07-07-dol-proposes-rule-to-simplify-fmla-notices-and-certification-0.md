## DOL Proposes Rule to Simplify FMLA Notices and Certifications

On June 30, 2026, the Department of Labor (DOL) proposed a new rule to simplify the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) notice and certification requirements. The proposed changes aim to reduce administrative burdens on employers and improve clarity for employees seeking leave. The proposed rule is open for public comment until August 31, 2026.

### Key Changes in the Proposed Rule

The proposed rule seeks to streamline the FMLA process by revising the forms used for notices and certifications. The DOL aims to make these forms more user-friendly and less time-consuming to complete. The proposal includes:

- **Standardized Electronic Forms**: Employers will be able to use standardized electronic forms for FMLA notices and certifications, reducing paperwork and facilitating easier recordkeeping.
- **Clearer Instructions**: The forms will include clearer instructions for both employers and employees to minimize misunderstandings and errors in the FMLA process.
- **Simplified Medical Certification**: The medical certification form will be simplified to focus on essential information needed to determine FMLA eligibility, reducing the burden on healthcare providers.

### Implications for Employers

Employers should prepare for potential changes by reviewing their current FMLA processes and considering how electronic forms might integrate into their existing systems. While the rule is not yet finalized, the following action items are recommended:

- **Evaluate Current FMLA Processes**: Assess how your organization currently handles FMLA notices and certifications. Identify areas where electronic forms could improve efficiency.
- **Engage with HR Software Providers**: If you use HR software, consult with your provider about integrating standardized electronic forms once the rule is finalized.
- **Train HR Staff**: Once the rule is finalized, ensure HR staff are trained on the new forms and processes to ensure compliance and minimize disruptions.

### Public Comment and Next Steps

The DOL is seeking public comment on the proposed rule until August 31, 2026. Employers, HR professionals, and other stakeholders are encouraged to provide feedback. Comments can be submitted electronically through the Federal eRulemaking Portal or by mail.

Following the comment period, the DOL will review the feedback and make any necessary revisions before issuing a final rule. The timeline for the final rule's implementation will depend on the volume and nature of the comments received.

Employers should stay alert for updates from the DOL regarding the final rule and prepare to adjust their FMLA processes accordingly. The proposed changes aim to make FMLA compliance more straightforward and less burdensome, benefiting both employers and employees.

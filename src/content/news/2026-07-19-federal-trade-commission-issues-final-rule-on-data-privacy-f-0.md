## New FTC Rule on Employee Data Privacy

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) has issued a final rule mandating stricter data privacy measures for employee records, effective January 1, 2027. This rule, published in the Federal Register on July 15, 2026, aims to enhance the protection of personal information collected by employers, aligning with the growing emphasis on data privacy across various sectors.

## Key Provisions

The rule requires employers to implement comprehensive data protection policies and procedures tailored to the sensitivity of the employee data they collect. Specifically, businesses must ensure that employee records are secured against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. This includes implementing encryption, regular data audits, and employee training on data privacy practices.

Additionally, the rule mandates that employers provide clear and conspicuous notices to employees regarding the types of personal information collected, the purposes for which it is used, and the measures taken to protect it. Employers must also establish protocols for responding to data breaches, including notifying affected individuals within 72 hours of discovering a breach.

## Impact on Employers

This rule impacts all employers covered by the FTC's jurisdiction, which includes most private sector employers except those in certain financial and telecommunications sectors. Employers with 50 or more employees are particularly urged to review their existing data privacy practices to ensure compliance with the new requirements.

Non-compliance with the FTC's rule could result in significant penalties, with fines up to $50,000 per violation. The FTC has indicated it will actively monitor compliance and enforce the rule through audits and investigations.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Review and Update Data Privacy Policies:** Employers should conduct a thorough review of their current data privacy policies and procedures. This includes updating security measures and ensuring that all employee records are adequately protected.

2. **Employee Training:** Implement mandatory training sessions for employees on data privacy and security practices. This will help minimize the risk of data breaches and ensure that all staff members are aware of their roles in protecting personal information.

3. **Breach Response Plan:** Develop or update a breach response plan that includes detailed steps for addressing data breaches and notifying affected individuals within the required timeframe.

4. **Legal Consultation:** Consider consulting with legal counsel to ensure that data privacy policies comply with the new FTC rule and align with other applicable laws and regulations.

This new rule underscores the importance of data privacy in the workplace and the need for employers to proactively safeguard employee information. With the effective date approaching, businesses should act promptly to address these requirements and avoid potential penalties.

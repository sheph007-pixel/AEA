## FTC Proposes New Guidelines on Influencer Marketing Disclosures

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) proposed new guidelines on June 29, 2026, aimed at tightening disclosure requirements for influencer marketing. These proposed changes could significantly impact how small businesses engage with influencers for advertising purposes.

Under the proposed guidelines, the FTC seeks to clarify and expand the rules around endorsements and testimonials in advertising, specifically targeting the influencer marketing industry. The guidelines emphasize transparency and honesty, requiring influencers to clearly disclose any material connections to brands they promote. This includes financial compensation, free products, or any other benefits received in exchange for endorsements.

The proposed changes build upon the existing guidelines outlined in the FTC's Endorsement Guides, which were last updated in 2009. The FTC aims to address the rapid growth of social media platforms and the increasing influence of online personalities in shaping consumer behavior. The new guidelines are intended to ensure that consumers are not misled by undisclosed paid endorsements.

### Key Changes

1. **Clearer Disclosure Requirements**: Influencers must use unambiguous language to disclose their relationships with brands. Vague terms like "partner" or "collab" may no longer suffice. The FTC suggests using explicit language such as "paid ad" or "sponsored by."

2. **Platform-Specific Guidance**: The guidelines provide specific recommendations for disclosures on various platforms, including Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. For instance, disclosures should be visible without requiring users to click "more" or "see more."

3. **Responsibility on Brands**: The proposed rules place greater responsibility on brands to ensure compliance. Businesses must educate influencers on disclosure requirements and monitor their content for compliance.

### Action Items for Employers

- **Review Influencer Contracts**: Businesses should audit existing contracts with influencers to ensure they include clear requirements for FTC-compliant disclosures. Amend contracts as necessary to align with the new guidelines.

- **Educate Influencers**: Provide training or resources to influencers on the updated disclosure requirements. Consider creating a compliance checklist or guide to aid influencers in adhering to the rules.

- **Monitor Compliance**: Implement a monitoring system to regularly review influencer content for compliance. Document efforts to ensure compliance as a safeguard against potential FTC investigations.

The FTC's proposed guidelines are open for public comment until August 31, 2026. Businesses involved in influencer marketing should consider submitting feedback or concerns during this period. Once finalized, these guidelines will become a critical component of compliance for any business leveraging influencer marketing strategies.

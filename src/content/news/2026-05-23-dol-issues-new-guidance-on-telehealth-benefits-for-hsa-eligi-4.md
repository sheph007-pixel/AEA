## New Guidance on Telehealth Benefits

The Department of Labor (DOL) released new guidance on May 20, 2026, clarifying the coverage of telehealth services under Health Savings Account (HSA)-eligible high-deductible health plans (HDHPs). This guidance, issued in collaboration with the Treasury Department, impacts employers offering HSA-eligible plans and aims to address confusion about telehealth benefits' compatibility with HSA rules.

## Key Details

Under the Internal Revenue Code § 223(c)(2), HDHPs must meet specific deductible requirements before covering non-preventive services. The new DOL guidance confirms that telehealth services can be covered without a deductible or at a reduced deductible without disqualifying the plan from being HSA-eligible, provided these services are preventive or meet certain criteria.

The guidance stems from the need to adapt to the increasing reliance on telehealth services, especially post-pandemic, and recognizes the role of telehealth in preventive care. This clarification helps ensure that employees can access telehealth services without compromising their ability to contribute to an HSA.

## Action Items for Employers

Employers who offer HSA-eligible HDHPs should review their plan designs to ensure compliance with the new guidance. Specifically, they should:

1. **Assess Current Telehealth Coverage**: Determine if current telehealth offerings align with the new guidance, ensuring preventive services are covered at no or reduced cost.

2. **Update Plan Documents**: Amend plan documents to reflect any changes in telehealth coverage and ensure they are communicated to employees clearly.

3. **Communicate with Employees**: Provide clear information to employees about the availability of telehealth services and how these may impact their HSA contributions.

4. **Consult with Benefits Advisors**: Work with benefits advisors or legal counsel to ensure that any modifications to telehealth benefits comply with the latest guidance and do not inadvertently disqualify the plan from HSA eligibility.

## Implications for Employers

This guidance provides greater flexibility for employers to offer telehealth services as part of their benefits package, potentially increasing employee satisfaction and health outcomes. By aligning telehealth benefits with HSA-eligible plans, employers can enhance their benefits offerings while maintaining tax advantages for employees.

Employers should act promptly to adjust their health plans in accordance with the new guidance, ensuring that they continue to offer competitive and compliant healthcare benefits.

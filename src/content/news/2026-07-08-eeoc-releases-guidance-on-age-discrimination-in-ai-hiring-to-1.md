## EEOC Issues New Guidance

On July 1, 2026, the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) released new guidance addressing age discrimination risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in hiring processes. This guidance aims to help employers understand potential pitfalls and ensure compliance with the Age Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967 (ADEA), which prohibits employment discrimination against individuals 40 years of age or older.

## Potential Risks in AI Hiring

The EEOC's guidance highlights how AI tools, which are increasingly used to streamline hiring, can inadvertently introduce age bias. Algorithms trained on biased data or designed without considering age-related factors may disproportionately screen out older candidates. The commission warns that reliance on such tools without proper oversight could lead to violations of the ADEA.

## Employer Action Items

Employers utilizing AI in hiring should take immediate steps to mitigate risks of age discrimination. First, conduct audits of AI tools to ensure they do not unfairly disadvantage older applicants. Collaborate with vendors to understand the data and methodologies used in AI systems. Additionally, provide training for HR personnel on recognizing and addressing potential biases in AI outputs.

The EEOC recommends implementing a review process for AI-driven hiring decisions to ensure compliance with anti-discrimination laws. Employers should also consider involving legal counsel in assessing the legality and fairness of AI tools in their recruitment processes.

## Legal Implications and Compliance

Failure to address age bias in AI hiring tools can result in legal challenges under the ADEA. The EEOC has signaled increased scrutiny of AI practices, and employers found in violation may face penalties, including fines and mandatory corrective measures.

Employers should remain vigilant in monitoring developments in AI and employment law. Regular updates to hiring policies and practices are crucial to maintaining compliance and avoiding potential litigation.

## Conclusion

The EEOC's new guidance underscores the importance of vigilance in the use of AI hiring tools. By proactively addressing potential age discrimination risks, employers can protect themselves from legal repercussions and foster a fair hiring environment.

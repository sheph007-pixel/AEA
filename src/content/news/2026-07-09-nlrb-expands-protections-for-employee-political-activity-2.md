## NLRB Decision on Political Expression

On June 30, 2026, the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) issued a decision in the case of *Smith & Co. v. NLRB* that expands protections for employee political activity under the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA). The Board ruled that employees engaging in political expression related to workplace issues are protected from employer retaliation. This decision impacts employers nationwide, requiring them to reassess their policies on political expression in the workplace.

## Implications for Employers

The NLRB's decision clarifies that political activities, such as participating in rallies or expressing views on social media, are protected when they relate to employment conditions or labor rights. This includes issues like minimum wage, workplace safety, and labor policies. Employers must understand that disciplining employees for such activities could now be considered a violation of the NLRA.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Review Policies**: Employers should review and update their employee handbooks and policies regarding political expression to ensure compliance with the new NLRB standards.
   
2. **Train Management**: Conduct training sessions for managers and supervisors to help them understand the expanded protections and avoid potential violations when addressing political activity in the workplace.

3. **Monitor Disciplinary Actions**: Carefully evaluate any disciplinary actions related to employee political expression to ensure they do not infringe on rights protected under the NLRA.

This decision marks a significant shift in how political expression is treated in the workplace, emphasizing the need for employers to align their practices with the NLRB's expanded interpretation of the NLRA.

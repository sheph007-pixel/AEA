## IRS Increases 401(k) Contribution Limits for 2026

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) announced an increase in the annual contribution limits for 401(k) plans, effective January 1, 2026. Under the new guidelines, employees can contribute up to $23,000 to their 401(k) accounts, up from the current limit of $22,500. This adjustment reflects the IRS's ongoing efforts to account for inflation and encourage retirement savings.

## Impact on Employers

Employers sponsoring 401(k) plans must update payroll systems to accommodate the new contribution limits. HR departments should communicate these changes to employees, emphasizing the benefits of maximizing retirement contributions. Additionally, employers should review and adjust any matching contribution formulas to ensure compliance with the increased limits.

## Compliance and Communication

Employers are required to update plan documentation and employee handbooks to reflect the new contribution limits. The IRS mandates that plan sponsors provide participants with a summary of material modifications (SMM) detailing these changes. Failure to comply with these requirements can result in penalties.

HR professionals should organize informational sessions or distribute newsletters to educate employees about the increased limits and the potential impact on their retirement savings. It's an opportunity to reinforce the value of participating in employer-sponsored retirement plans.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Update Payroll Systems**: Ensure payroll systems are configured to handle the increased contribution limits effective January 1, 2026.

2. **Review Matching Contributions**: Adjust employer matching contributions as necessary to align with the new limits.

3. **Communicate Changes**: Distribute SMMs and update plan documents to reflect the new contribution limits. Inform employees through newsletters or informational sessions.

4. **Plan Document Review**: Ensure all plan documents are compliant with the new IRS guidelines to avoid potential penalties.

The IRS's adjustment to 401(k) contribution limits offers employees an enhanced opportunity to boost their retirement savings. Employers must act swiftly to ensure compliance and communicate effectively with their workforce.

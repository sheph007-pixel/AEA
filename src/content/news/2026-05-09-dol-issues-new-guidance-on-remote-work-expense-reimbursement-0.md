## DOL Clarifies Remote Work Expense Obligations

On May 5, 2026, the Department of Labor (DOL) issued new guidance clarifying employer obligations to reimburse remote work expenses under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). This guidance is crucial for businesses with remote or hybrid workforces to ensure compliance with federal wage laws.

The DOL's guidance addresses whether employers must reimburse employees for costs incurred while working from home, such as internet access, office supplies, and other business-related expenses. According to the DOL, employers are required to reimburse these expenses if failing to do so would effectively reduce an employee's earnings below the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour (29 U.S.C. § 206(a)(1)).

## Key Points for Employers

The guidance specifies that any necessary work-related expense that an employee incurs should not bring their net earnings below the minimum wage threshold. Employers must evaluate whether the costs employees pay out of pocket for remote work-related expenses result in earnings below this threshold.

Employers are not required to reimburse all remote work expenses but must consider the impact of such expenses on an employee's take-home pay. For example, if an employee earning $8 per hour incurs $50 per month in internet expenses solely for work purposes, the employer must reimburse this amount to maintain compliance with the FLSA.

## Action Items for Employers

Employers should take the following steps to ensure compliance with the DOL's guidance:

1. **Review Compensation Structures**: Audit current pay rates and ensure that any deductions for remote work expenses do not reduce employee earnings below the federal minimum wage.

2. **Implement Reimbursement Policies**: Establish clear policies for reimbursing remote work expenses, specifying what expenses qualify and the process for reimbursement.

3. **Communicate with Employees**: Inform employees about the reimbursement policy and how they can claim expenses. Ensure transparency in the process to avoid misunderstandings.

4. **Document Expenses**: Maintain records of all reimbursed expenses to demonstrate compliance in the event of a DOL audit.

Employers should act promptly to review and adjust their policies to align with the DOL's guidance. Non-compliance could lead to investigations and potential penalties under the FLSA. By addressing these requirements, businesses can support their remote workforce while minimizing legal risks.

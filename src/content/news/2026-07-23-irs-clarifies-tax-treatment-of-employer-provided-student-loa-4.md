## IRS Issues New Guidance on Student Loan Repayment Benefits

The IRS released new guidance on July 15, 2026, clarifying the tax treatment of employer-provided student loan repayment assistance under Section 127 of the Internal Revenue Code. The guidance aims to resolve ambiguities about the tax implications of these benefits, which have become increasingly popular among employers seeking to attract and retain talent.

## Key Details of the IRS Guidance

The IRS confirmed that up to $5,250 per year in employer-provided student loan repayment assistance can be excluded from an employee's taxable income under Section 127. This exclusion applies to payments made directly to the lender or reimbursed to the employee, provided that the assistance is part of a formal educational assistance program.

To qualify for the tax exclusion, employers must ensure their educational assistance programs meet specific criteria outlined in the IRS guidance. These include having a written plan, ensuring the program does not favor highly compensated employees, and providing reasonable notification of the program's availability to all eligible employees.

## Action Items for Employers

Employers offering or planning to offer student loan repayment assistance should review their existing educational assistance programs to ensure compliance with the IRS's requirements. Key actions include:

1. **Formalize the Program**: Ensure that the student loan repayment assistance is part of a written educational assistance plan. The plan should clearly outline eligibility criteria and the process for receiving benefits.

2. **Review Benefit Limits**: Confirm that the total assistance provided does not exceed the $5,250 annual limit to maintain tax-exempt status for employees.

3. **Ensure Non-Discrimination**: Verify that the program does not disproportionately benefit highly compensated employees. This may require a review of employee classifications and benefit distributions.

4. **Communicate with Employees**: Clearly communicate the availability and terms of the student loan repayment assistance program to all eligible employees. This can be done through employee handbooks, emails, or company intranet postings.

5. **Maintain Documentation**: Keep detailed records of all payments made under the program and documentation supporting compliance with IRS requirements. This is crucial in case of an audit.

## Implications for Employers

The IRS guidance provides a clearer framework for employers to offer tax-advantaged student loan repayment assistance, a benefit that has gained traction as student debt levels remain high. By adhering to the outlined requirements, employers can enhance their compensation packages while providing meaningful financial support to employees.

With this clarification, employers can confidently integrate student loan repayment assistance into their benefits offerings, potentially improving recruitment and retention efforts in a competitive job market. As always, consulting with tax professionals or legal counsel is advisable to ensure full compliance with the latest IRS regulations.

## FTC Targets Deceptive Online Practices

On July 7, 2026, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) proposed new guidelines aimed at curbing the use of "dark patterns" in online transactions. These deceptive design practices manipulate users into making unintended purchases or sharing more information than they intended. The proposal comes amid increasing scrutiny over online consumer protection and follows a series of high-profile complaints about misleading interfaces on popular e-commerce platforms.

## What the Proposed Guidelines Entail

The proposed guidelines define dark patterns as user interface designs that trick or manipulate consumers into actions they might not otherwise take. These can include hidden charges, disguised ads, or misleading countdown timers that pressure users into quick decisions. The FTC's proposal outlines specific practices that would be deemed unfair or deceptive under Section 5 of the FTC Act (15 U.S.C. § 45).

## Implications for Employers and HR Professionals

Businesses operating online platforms should closely review these proposed guidelines to ensure compliance and avoid potential penalties. The FTC's focus is broad, covering any digital interface that could mislead consumers. This includes checkout processes, subscription sign-ups, and privacy settings. Companies should audit their digital interfaces for any elements that could be considered manipulative under the new guidelines.

## Action Items for Businesses

1. **Conduct a UX Audit**: Review your website and app interfaces for elements that could be classified as dark patterns. Pay special attention to subscription services and e-commerce checkout processes.

2. **Update Privacy Policies**: Ensure that privacy settings and data collection practices are transparent and easy for users to understand and adjust.

3. **Train Design and Marketing Teams**: Educate your teams about the new guidelines and the importance of ethical design practices.

4. **Consult Legal Counsel**: Work with legal experts to interpret the guidelines and ensure your business practices align with regulatory expectations.

## Next Steps

The public comment period for the proposed guidelines is open until September 30, 2026. Businesses are encouraged to submit feedback or concerns regarding the potential impact of these guidelines on their operations. The FTC aims to finalize the guidelines by early 2027, with enforcement actions expected to follow shortly after.

Staying ahead of these changes will be crucial for businesses to avoid legal challenges and maintain consumer trust. As regulatory scrutiny intensifies, transparency and user-centric design will be key to navigating the evolving landscape of online business operations.

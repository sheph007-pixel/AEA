## IRS Clarifies Employee Retention Credit Eligibility

On May 15, 2026, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) issued updated guidance on the Employee Retention Credit (ERC), impacting businesses' eligibility assessments and tax filings. The new guidelines address ambiguities in prior regulations and clarify the types of wages and expenses that qualify for the credit under the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security (CARES) Act, as amended by subsequent legislation.

The ERC was introduced to help businesses retain employees during the COVID-19 pandemic by offering a refundable tax credit against certain employment taxes. The recent IRS update provides clarity on eligibility criteria, particularly concerning the definition of "qualified wages" and the impact of other relief programs on ERC eligibility.

### Key Changes in the Guidance

The IRS's updated guidance specifies that wages paid to related individuals, as defined under 26 U.S.C. § 51(i)(1), do not qualify for the credit. This clarification affects businesses employing family members and necessitates a review of payroll records to ensure compliance.

Additionally, the guidance addresses the interaction between the ERC and the Paycheck Protection Program (PPP). Employers who received PPP loans can also claim the ERC, but not for the same wages used to justify PPP loan forgiveness. This distinction requires careful allocation of wages to maximize benefits from both programs.

### Action Items for Employers

1. **Review Payroll Records:** Employers should audit payroll records to identify wages paid to related individuals and exclude them from ERC calculations. This step is crucial to avoid potential penalties and ensure accurate tax filings.

2. **Coordinate Relief Benefits:** Businesses that received PPP loans must carefully allocate wages between ERC claims and PPP loan forgiveness applications. Consider consulting with a tax professional to optimize the use of both relief measures.

3. **Amend Tax Filings if Necessary:** If prior filings included ineligible wages, employers may need to amend their tax returns. The IRS provides procedures for correcting previously filed Forms 941, the Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return.

4. **Stay Updated on Further Guidance:** As the IRS continues to refine its guidance, employers should monitor updates to remain compliant and take full advantage of available credits.

This updated guidance aims to simplify the process for businesses claiming the ERC and ensure that the credit is applied correctly. By addressing these clarifications, employers can better manage their tax liabilities and optimize the financial support available through the ERC.

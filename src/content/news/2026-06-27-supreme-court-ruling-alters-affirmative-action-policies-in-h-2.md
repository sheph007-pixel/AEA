## Supreme Court Decision

On June 26, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled in the case of Students for Fair Admissions v. Harvard College, significantly impacting affirmative action policies in employment. The Court's decision restricts the use of race as a factor in hiring, aligning with its recent stance on college admissions. This ruling affects all employers who currently incorporate affirmative action policies in their hiring processes, particularly those with federal contracts.

## Implications for Employers

The ruling directly challenges existing practices under Executive Order 11246, which mandates affirmative action and prohibits federal contractors from discriminating based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or national origin. Employers must now reassess their hiring policies to ensure compliance with the new legal landscape.

The decision also raises questions about the future of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission's (EEOC) guidelines on affirmative action in hiring. Employers should anticipate potential updates to these guidelines and prepare for increased scrutiny of their hiring practices.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Review Hiring Policies**: Employers should immediately audit their hiring policies and procedures to ensure they do not rely on race as a deciding factor in employment decisions. This includes revisiting diversity initiatives that could be perceived as race-based preferences.

2. **Consult Legal Counsel**: Given the complexity of the ruling, it is advisable for employers to consult with legal counsel to understand its implications fully and to navigate any necessary changes to their employment practices.

3. **Training and Communication**: HR professionals and hiring managers should be trained on the implications of the ruling to prevent inadvertent non-compliance. Clear communication regarding policy changes should be disseminated throughout the organization.

4. **Monitor EEOC Updates**: Stay alert for any updates from the EEOC regarding affirmative action guidelines. Changes to federal guidance could further influence employer obligations and best practices.

## Future Considerations

The Supreme Court's decision may lead to further legal challenges and legislative responses. Employers should be prepared for potential shifts in state laws that might offer different or additional requirements regarding diversity and inclusion in hiring practices. As the legal landscape evolves, maintaining flexibility and readiness to adapt will be crucial for compliance and effective talent management.

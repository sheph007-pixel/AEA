## DOL's New Rule on Remote Work Expenses

The Department of Labor (DOL) has issued a new rule requiring employers to reimburse certain remote work expenses, effective June 1, 2026. This rule, under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), mandates that employers cover necessary expenses incurred by employees working from home. 

The rule specifies that costs directly related to performing work duties, such as internet service, office supplies, and necessary equipment, must be reimbursed if these expenses bring an employee's earnings below the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour (29 U.S.C. § 206). The DOL emphasizes that this measure aims to ensure that employees are not financially disadvantaged by the shift to remote work.

## What Employers Need to Do

Employers should immediately review their remote work policies to ensure compliance with the new rule. Key actions include:

- **Audit Current Expenses:** Determine which expenses are necessary for employees to perform their duties remotely. This includes internet access, computer equipment, and any other tools essential for job performance.
  
- **Implement Reimbursement Procedures:** Establish a clear process for employees to submit reimbursement requests. This should include guidelines on what constitutes a reimbursable expense and the documentation required for approval.

- **Communicate with Employees:** Clearly communicate the new reimbursement policy to all remote workers. Ensure employees understand what expenses are covered and how to submit claims.

- **Budget Adjustments:** Adjust budgets to account for the increased costs associated with reimbursing remote work expenses. This may require reallocating funds from other areas or seeking additional resources.

## Compliance and Enforcement

The DOL will monitor compliance through audits and employee complaints. Employers found in violation of the rule may face penalties, including back pay for unpaid expenses and additional fines. To avoid these consequences, employers should maintain detailed records of all reimbursed expenses and ensure transparency in their reimbursement processes.

The new rule reflects the ongoing evolution of workplace norms, as remote work becomes increasingly prevalent. By ensuring employees are not financially burdened by remote work requirements, the DOL aims to promote fair labor practices in the modern work environment. For employers, this rule underscores the importance of adapting to new regulatory requirements and maintaining equitable treatment of all employees, regardless of their work location.

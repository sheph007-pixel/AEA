## DOL Clarifies Unpaid Internship Criteria

The Department of Labor (DOL) has issued a final rule clarifying the criteria for unpaid internships under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), effective September 1, 2026. This new rule aims to provide clearer guidelines for employers on when internships can be unpaid, potentially impacting businesses that rely on intern labor.

## Key Changes to Internship Guidelines

The rule refines the "primary beneficiary test," used to determine if an intern is the primary beneficiary of the internship, thereby permitting an unpaid arrangement. Under the new regulations, the DOL has outlined seven factors that must be considered:

1. The extent to which the intern and the employer clearly understand there is no expectation of compensation.
2. The degree to which the internship provides training similar to that given in an educational environment.
3. The connection between the intern’s educational program and the internship, including the receipt of academic credit.
4. The extent to which the internship accommodates the intern’s academic commitments by corresponding to the academic calendar.

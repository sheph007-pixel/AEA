## NLRB Ruling on Email Use

On May 31, 2026, the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) issued a decision reaffirming employees' rights to use work email systems for union organizing activities. This decision, in the case of XYZ Corp. v. NLRB, reverses a previous 2019 ruling that restricted such use, aligning with the 2014 Purple Communications standard.

Under the new ruling, employees at companies where email is a regular communication tool may use their work email for union-related communications during non-working time. The decision emphasizes that employers cannot prohibit such usage unless they can demonstrate special circumstances that justify a ban.

## Implications for Employers

Employers must now allow union-related emails on their systems unless they can prove that such activities significantly disrupt business operations. The burden is on the employer to show that any restrictions are necessary to maintain workplace efficiency and discipline.

This ruling affects all employers subject to the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA), particularly those with established email systems as a primary mode of employee communication. The decision underscores the NLRA's protection of employees' rights to engage in concerted activities for mutual aid and protection.

## Action Items

1. **Review Email Policies:** Employers should immediately review and, if necessary, revise their email usage policies to ensure compliance with the NLRB's decision. Policies should clearly state that employees may use email for union activities during non-working hours unless legitimate business reasons dictate otherwise.

2. **Train Management and HR:** Conduct training sessions for managers and HR personnel to ensure they understand the implications of this ruling and can handle union-related communications appropriately.

3. **Document Special Circumstances:** If an employer believes there are special circumstances that justify restricting email use, these must be documented thoroughly. This documentation should clearly outline how union-related email use would disrupt operations or violate legal obligations.

4. **Monitor Compliance:** Regularly audit email usage policies and practices to ensure ongoing compliance with NLRB standards and to address any potential issues proactively.

By taking these steps, employers can align their practices with the current legal framework and avoid potential legal challenges. This decision reflects a broader trend of reinforcing employee rights in the workplace, and employers should stay updated on further developments in labor law.

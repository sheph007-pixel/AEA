## DOL Releases New Guidance on Mental Health Accommodations

The Department of Labor (DOL) issued new guidance on July 8, 2026, detailing employer obligations under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) concerning mental health accommodations. This guidance clarifies the steps employers must take to accommodate employees with mental health conditions, emphasizing the need for individualized assessments and reasonable accommodations.

The ADA requires employers to provide reasonable accommodations to qualified individuals with disabilities, unless doing so would cause undue hardship. The new guidance highlights that mental health conditions, such as depression, anxiety, and PTSD, can qualify as disabilities under the ADA. Employers must engage in an interactive process with employees to determine appropriate accommodations.

## Key Points for Employers

The DOL's guidance stresses that employers should not dismiss requests for accommodations related to mental health conditions. Instead, they should actively participate in discussions with employees to identify effective solutions. Possible accommodations may include flexible work schedules, telecommuting options, or modified job duties.

Employers are also reminded of the importance of confidentiality. Information about an employee's mental health condition should be kept private and only shared with those directly involved in the accommodation process.

## Action Items for Employers

1. **Review Accommodation Policies**: Ensure that your company's accommodation policies explicitly address mental health conditions. Update policies to reflect the DOL's guidance.

2. **Train Managers and HR Staff**: Provide training on the interactive process and confidentiality requirements. Managers and HR personnel should be equipped to handle accommodation requests sensitively and effectively.

3. **Document the Process**: Keep detailed records of all accommodation requests and the steps taken to address them. Documentation can be crucial in demonstrating compliance with the ADA.

4. **Consult Legal Counsel**: Given the complexities of ADA compliance, consulting with legal counsel can help ensure that your policies and practices align with the latest guidance.

The DOL's new guidance on mental health accommodations underscores the evolving understanding of disabilities in the workplace. By taking proactive steps, employers can foster an inclusive environment that supports the mental health of all employees.

## New H-1B Visa Wage Rule Takes Effect

The United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) implemented a new wage rule for H-1B visas on May 1, 2026. This rule changes the wage requirements for H-1B workers, impacting employers who rely on foreign talent. The final rule, published in the Federal Register, aims to ensure that wages for H-1B workers are commensurate with the prevailing wages of their respective occupations.

### Key Changes

The new rule modifies the wage levels used to determine the minimum salary an employer must pay H-1B workers. Specifically, it adjusts the calculation of prevailing wage levels, which are categorized into four tiers based on experience and skill requirements. The changes are intended to better reflect the actual wages paid to U.S. workers in similar positions.

Under the new rule, the wage levels are set closer to the median wage for each occupation and geographic area. This adjustment is expected to increase the salary requirements for H-1B workers, particularly for positions that fall under the lower wage levels.

### Implications for Employers

Employers must now ensure compliance with the updated wage levels when filing H-1B petitions. Failure to meet the new wage requirements could result in petition denials or penalties. This change may affect the budget planning for companies that heavily depend on H-1B workers, particularly in industries such as technology and engineering.

### Action Items

1. **Review Current H-1B Salaries**: Employers should audit the salaries of their current H-1B employees to ensure they meet the new wage levels. Adjustments may be necessary to comply with the updated requirements.

2. **Update Future Petitions**: When preparing new H-1B petitions, employers must use the revised wage levels to determine the appropriate salary offerings. This includes both initial petitions and extensions.

3. **Consult Legal Experts**: Given the complexity of immigration regulations, consulting with immigration attorneys or specialists is advisable to navigate the changes effectively and avoid potential legal issues.

### Conclusion

The new H-1B wage rule represents a significant shift in how employers must approach compensation for foreign workers. By aligning H-1B wages more closely with local labor market conditions, the rule seeks to protect U.S. workers while maintaining the integrity of the H-1B program. Employers must act promptly to adjust their practices to comply with these changes.
